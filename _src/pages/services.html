Our process

From First Survey
to Final Key

Four phases, one accountable team. Each phase closes with a clear report, so you always know where the project and the budget stand.

  1. Feasibility & planning

    Site surveys, market analysis, zoning checks and a feasibility study that tests the numbers.

  2. Design & approvals

    Architectural and engineering design, costed bills of quantities and council approvals.

  3. Build

    Foundations, structure, services and finishes, managed daily against programme and budget.

  4. Handover

    Snagging, commissioning, documentation and the keys, with after-care support.
