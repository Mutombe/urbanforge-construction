Our story

Building Landmarks
Across Zimbabwe

Founded with a vision to redefine the property landscape in Zimbabwe, Urban Forge Construction has grown into a leading force in property development and large-scale construction.

Operating across Harare, Mutare and Bulawayo, we manage a diverse portfolio worth over USD 5 million. We don't just build structures; we create sustainable ecosystems for businesses to thrive and families to live comfortably.

Our foundation is deep local knowledge paired with world-class engineering standards. When you partner with Urban Forge, you are partnering with a commanding presence in the industry.

  • End-to-end delivery, from raw land to key handover
  • ISO 9001 registered with the Standards Association of Zimbabwe
  • Transparent reporting at every stage of the build
