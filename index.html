What services does Urban Forge provide?
We cover the full lifecycle of a development: property development (feasibility, design, financing structuring and execution), construction services, project management, and land development, turning raw land into serviced, investment-ready stands.
Where in Zimbabwe do you operate?
Our headquarters is at 11 Falmouth Road, Alexander Park, Harare, and we deliver projects across Harare, Mutare and Bulawayo. We're happy to discuss sites elsewhere in the country.
Can you take a project from raw land to handover?
Yes. We manage surveying and zoning, install roads, water and power, then design, build and hand over, so you deal with one accountable partner from start to finish.
How do you keep projects on time and on budget?
Through rigorous oversight, disciplined contractor management and transparent reporting. You always know what has been spent, what has been built and what comes next.
How do you manage quality and safety?
We are an ISO 9001 registered organisation with the Standards Association of Zimbabwe, and every structural element is built to meet global standards for safety, durability and finish.
